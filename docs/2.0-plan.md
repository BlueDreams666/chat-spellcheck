# 💖 Chat Spellcheck 2.0 plan

Goal: a big, polished upgrade that feels great on day one for new players, while staying
client-side, offline, fast, and working on both Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3.

## ✅ In scope

### 1. Smarter spellchecking
- **Better suggestions in 6 more languages.** Add word-frequency ranking for German, Swiss German,
  Spanish, French, Italian and Hebrew. The data is SymSpell's frequency lists, which have the same
  source and licence as the English list we already ship (Google Books Ngram, CC BY 3.0, with
  SymSpell under MIT). Each list is about 1 MB, and it only loads when its language is on.
  Measured with new ranking tests, like the English ones.
- **Fewer false alarms on chat-speak.**
  - Stretched words like `sooo`, `yesss`, `hiii` and `noooo` are accepted when the shorter word is
    a real word.
  - Laughs and keyboard smashes like `hahaha`, `lmaooo` and `xddd` aren't flagged.
  - More gamer slang goes on the allow-list: `ez`, `op`, `nerf`, `pvp`, `smp`, `noob`,
    `ngl`, `fr` and others.
- **Names are left alone.** A capitalised word in the middle of a sentence ("ask Zorblax") isn't
  underlined if it's unknown. On by default, with an option to turn it off. A typo at the start
  of a sentence is still caught.

### 2. A friendly first run
- **Picks your language automatically.** On first launch, the mod turns on the dictionary that
  matches Minecraft's language (for example `es_mx` gives Spanish and `en_gb` gives English (UK)).
  English (US) stays the fallback.
- **Welcome tip.** The first few times you open chat, a small pink tip says: "♥ Hover a red word
  or press Ctrl+. for fixes · /spellcheck for settings". It goes away for good after you fix
  your first typo or open chat three times. You can bring it back from settings.

### 3. A cuter settings screen
- Pink theme to match the new icon: heart title, soft rounded panels, and pink checkboxes.
- **Grouped sections:**
  - **Languages** (the same grid, with a small "game language" badge on the one that matches)
  - **Checking** (pill-style ON/OFF switches for Spellcheck, Autocorrect, Check /msg text and
    Skip names)
  - **Look** (underline style and colour)
- **Live preview.** A fake chat line, "i love this mod its so helpfull ♥", that updates with your
  settings. It shows the squiggle in your chosen style and colour, nothing when spellcheck is
  off, and the autocorrected version when autocorrect is on.
- **Underline style and colour.** Choose squiggle, straight or dotted, and red, pink, orange or
  blue. Blue helps players with red-green colour blindness.
- **Readable at every GUI scale.** The screen scrolls when it doesn't fit (GUI scale 4 and small
  windows), and two columns become one when the window is narrow.

### 4. Little visualisations
- **Stats card in settings.**
  - Words checked, typos caught and typos fixed, for this session and all time.
  - A tiny 7-day bar chart of fixes.
  - Your "accuracy streak".
  - Everything is stored locally in `config/chatspellcheck/stats.properties`, and nothing is sent
    anywhere.
- **Gentle animations.**
  - New underlines draw in from left to right over about 120 ms.
  - The popover slides up 2 px as it fades in.
  - When you fix a word, it gets a short pink sparkle.
  - The game test's overlay-cost check must stay within its current budget.

### 5. Release polish
- Version 2.0.0, README and CHANGELOG updates, new screenshots, and before/after shots of the
  settings screen.
- Game tests for every new feature, plus screenshots, on both 26.2 and 26.3.

## 💤 Not in 2.0 (maybe later)
- **"Check before sending" (Enter twice to send with typos).** It could annoy people and fights
  with how chat normally works. I'd rather see feedback first.
- **Grammar, or "their vs there".** That needs sentence context, which is a much bigger project.
- **Frequency ranking for Dutch, Danish, Norwegian and Arabic.** There's no list with a clean
  licence yet, so these keep the current ranking.
- **Spellcheck on signs, books and anvils.**
